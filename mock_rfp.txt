REQUEST FOR PROPOSAL: Enterprise Supply Chain Modernization
Issued By: GlobalRetail Logistics Inc.
Date: September 2026

1. Project Overview
GlobalRetail Logistics is seeking an IT services partner to modernize our legacy supply chain infrastructure. We are experiencing a 15% delay in inventory routing due to manual data entry and fragmented on-premise servers. 

2. Technical Requirements
The selected vendor must provide a solution that includes:
- Migration of 50TB of on-premise inventory data to a secure Cloud environment (AWS or Azure).
- Development of a Machine Learning forecasting model to predict inventory shortages across our 400 retail locations.
- Implementation of an automated, AI-driven ticketing system for vendor management.

3. Compliance & Constraints
- All cloud infrastructure and data processing must comply with GDPR and SOC 2 Type II security standards.
- The project must be completed within a strict 6-month timeline to align with our Q1 fiscal targets.
- The total vendor budget is capped at $1.5M USD.
